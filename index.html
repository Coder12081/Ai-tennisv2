Setup: Mount the camera and keep the whole taped court in view. Press Calibrate court and tap the four outer corners clockwise. Then hold and drag the numbered handles to align them with the tape. Press Set net, tap both ends of the net, then tap on your side. The tracker will focus on that half. The line counts as in. Bounce calls use motion changes and are estimates from one camera view.

How this version works

The detector searches for saturated tennis-ball yellow, filters candidates by shape and apparent size, and follows the best candidate using motion prediction. The match score is a heuristic, not a probability.
