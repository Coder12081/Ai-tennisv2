How this version works

The detector learns the ball's HSV colour from your actual camera image, checks connected-component roundness, aspect ratio and area, and uses a constant-velocity prediction with a limited search window. This is still classical computer vision, not a trained neural network. The score is a matching heuristic, not a probability.
